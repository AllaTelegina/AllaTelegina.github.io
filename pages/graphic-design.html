Graphic Design

Brief

Design an A4 promotional flyer for Ellie.pro, a business assistance service. The goal was to present its services, brand values, and pricing plans, with a clear invitation to get in touch via Telegram.

Sketches / Design

I created three visual concepts in Figma, exploring different color palettes and layouts. Each version organizes the service list, brand values, and three pricing plans within a single A4 page.

The concepts use contrasting headings, directional icons, and distinct pricing cards to guide the reader through the offer. A contact call to action sits at the bottom of the page.

Tools & Technologies

Figma — A4 layout, typography, color exploration, and promotional design.

Final Result

All three A4 flyer versions were delivered to the client: black and yellow, blue and cream, and green with light panels.
