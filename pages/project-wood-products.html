Wood Products Website

A Figma website design for a birch plywood and wood products manufacturer.

Brief

The project is a commercial website designed for a company specializing in the production and sale of birch plywood and wood products. The main goal was to present the products in a clear, structured, and visually trustworthy way while emphasizing quality, eco-friendliness, and production reliability.

Special focus was placed on product quality, stable supply volumes, and transparent working conditions. The website is aimed at private customers, households, small businesses, and partners looking for a reliable supplier of natural wood products.

Sketches / Design

I explored several layout and color variations in Figma to find a suitable visual direction for the client. The concepts compare green and red accents, different arrangements of the main content, and ways to highlight the product range and enquiry form.

View Full Design ↗

During the design stage, wireframes and UI concepts were created in Figma, focusing on clear information hierarchy and conversion-oriented layouts. The homepage structure includes a hero section with a value proposition, product cards, company information, advantages, and a lead generation form.

The visual style is based on natural color accents — green, beige, and wood tones — communicating eco-friendliness, reliability, and a connection to nature. Card-based layouts and large buttons guide the user’s attention and simplify decision-making.

Tools & Technologies

The project was designed in Figma using Auto Layout for scalable components, grid systems for precise alignment, and reusable UI elements for product cards, advantages, and forms.

The layout was designed with further implementation in mind using HTML and Tailwind CSS for fast and adaptive styling, Alpine.js for lightweight interactivity, and backend integration for handling customer requests and product inquiries.

Responsiveness

The website design is fully responsive and optimized for desktop screens, laptops, tablets, and mobile devices. Product cards, text blocks, and forms remain clear and easy to use on any screen size.

Special attention was paid to mobile usability, as many users search for wood product suppliers and place requests directly from smartphones.

Final Result

The final result is a modern, trustworthy, and conversion-focused website that clearly communicates its value proposition: high-quality birch plywood, reliable production, and eco-friendly materials.

The project combines a strong visual identity, clear product presentation, and a user-friendly structure. The design can be effectively used as a foundation for a real commercial website in the wood production and supply industry.
