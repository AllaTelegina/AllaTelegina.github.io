Poster Design

Typography and composition studies for music and seasonal campaigns.

Brief

An academic exercise in poster design, exploring typography, color, and composition through a music festival poster and a seasonal promotional graphic.

Sketches / Design

The Music Fest poster uses stacked lettering and colored circles to establish a visual rhythm. The Hot Summer graphic combines iridescent shapes with contrasting black and white areas and compact supporting text.

Tools & Technologies

Adobe Photoshop

Final Result

Two finished graphics showing different approaches to hierarchy, contrast, and the relationship between text and abstract shapes.

Hot Summer

A seasonal graphic exploring iridescent shapes, overlapping forms, and contrasting typography.
